## Project Structure Rating: 10 / 10
 
The project is well organized. Its files are sorted into clear folders for the layout, the pages, and the reusable parts, which made it easy to find what I needed. The review section is kept in its own location rather than placed inside a page, which reflects thoughtful planning. File names such as Login, Register, Header, and Footer are simple and descriptive, and no file is excessively long. The commit history is a particular strength. The messages follow the conventional commits format, using labels like "feat" and "style" at the start of each entry, for example "feat(feedback): add interactive customer review component" and "style(ui): redesigned login and register page". This makes it simple to trace how the project developed over time. The author also noticed that some unnecessary build files had been uploaded by mistake and removed them promptly, which shows good attention to detail.
 
---
 
## Front-End Rating: 10 / 10
 
The branding is the first thing that stands out. The pixel-style font paired with an elegant serif, along with the black, white, and orange color scheme, gives the site the look of a genuine tech company. The translucent blurred header is clean, the opening section is bold, and the dark category area with product cards adds a welcome change of pace as you scroll. The login and register pages are especially well done. They feature a centered card, error messages placed directly under each field, a "LOGGING IN..." status on the button, and clear success and error notices, so they feel like polished, real-world forms. The Community Feedback section, with its clickable star ratings, makes the page feel interactive rather than purely visual. Buttons include a subtle press effect and spacing is consistent throughout, which gives the whole site a smooth and finished feel.
